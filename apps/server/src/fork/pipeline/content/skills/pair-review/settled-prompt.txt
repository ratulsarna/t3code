Settled. Each line below is a concern on this change that was already raised and
closed without a code fix, as the lead states it: the case the decision covers and
what the decision rests on. Do not report one again as it stands.

Report a settled concern when you bring something its line lacks: a case outside
the one it names; evidence that what it rests on is false or does not hold at this
commit; or evidence, from the code or from a run, that the harm is worse or easier
to reach than the line says. Say what is new.

The lines are claims, not facts. A line vaguer or wider than a decision could be,
or one whose basis is not what it says when you open it, is a finding.
